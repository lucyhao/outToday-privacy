Support

Questions, a bug to report, or something not working right? You're in the right place — check the answers below, or reach out directly and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send a reset link — open it, choose a new password, then return to GoOutToday and sign in.

I didn't get my confirmation or reset email

Check your spam folder first. If it's been more than a few minutes, wait a bit and try again — our email provider limits how many can go out in a short window. Still stuck? Email us below and we'll sort it out.

Why does GoOutToday need my location?

Location is used live, only while the app is open, to fetch local weather and find nearby places to recommend. We don't store a history of where you've been — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Where do I buy tickets for a place?

GoOutToday doesn't sell tickets directly — tap 🌐 Website on any place's detail page to go straight to the venue's own site, where you can check current prices and buy tickets if needed. It's also worth checking for holiday hours or special notices before you go.

How do I add or edit my kids' profiles?

Head to the Profile tab → My Kids. Tap a child to edit them, or Add Another Child to create a new profile.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account at the bottom of the page. This permanently removes your account and everything in it — this can't be undone.

The app is crashing or something's broken

Try force-quitting and reopening GoOutToday first. If that doesn't help, email us below with your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened — that helps us track it down fast.

I run a local event or venue — can I get featured?

We're always looking for family-friendly events and places to feature in the app's Events section. Email us below with the event or venue name, dates, location, and a link, and we'll take a look.
